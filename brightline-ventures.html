Our thesis

The biggest industries still run on manual work.

Power grids, factories, hospitals and banks keep the world running. Much of their daily work is still done by hand, on paper or in old systems. We back teams that use AI to automate that work, and that know how to get their product adopted by the people who use it every day.

For founders

Building AI for a real-world industry? We would like to hear from you.

What we look for

  • A real problem in a real industryWork that is slow, costly or error-prone today, in energy, manufacturing, healthcare or finance.
  • A product that is already in usePaying customers or serious pilots, and a clear sense of why they bought.
  • A team that knows the buyerFounders who understand how their customers make decisions and roll out new tools.
  • Room to grow across marketsA business that can expand across the US, Asia or Europe.

What we bring after we invest

  • Help with salesAdvice on selling into large, careful buyers, from first meeting to signed contract.
  • IntroductionsConnections to customers, partners and future hires in our sectors.
  • Support in new marketsPractical help when you expand into the US, Asia or Europe.
  • Money for later roundsReserved capital so we can keep backing you as you grow.
